About these numbers

Source: the supplied dashboard_counties.csv, described as California DOJ OpenJustice data. This page uses a manually embedded snapshot, not a live feed.

Clearance ratio = arrest and conviction records cleared ÷ historical arrests. This is not the percentage of eligible records cleared. Records are not unique people. The historical arrest period and reporting date were not supplied.

Disposition gap is shown as supplied. Its denominator and effect on relief eligibility require source documentation; it does not by itself establish that records cannot receive relief.

The comparison uses the unweighted average of all 58 counties. Rankings describe the supplied data and do not establish which agency is responsible for implementation gaps. Tied ratios share a rank. This basic version does not include the separate 2022–2024 annual dataset.
